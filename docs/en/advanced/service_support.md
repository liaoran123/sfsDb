# Service Support

## Overview

sfsDb adopts a service-oriented business model, providing customers with comprehensive technical support and services. Our service system aims to ensure that customers can smoothly use sfsDb, solve problems encountered during use, and receive continuous technical support.

## Service Levels

### 1. Community Service (Free)
- **Support Channels**: GitHub Issues
- **Response Time**: Community mutual assistance, no official commitment
- **Support Scope**: Basic usage issues, documentation consultation
- **Suitable For**: Individual developers, students, small projects

### 2. Professional Service (Paid)
- **Support Channels**: Email, phone, remote assistance
- **Response Time**: 24-hour response during working hours
- **Support Scope**: Technical problem troubleshooting, performance optimization, configuration guidance
- **Charging Method**: Per-time or monthly subscription

### 3. Enterprise Service (Paid)
- **Support Channels**: Dedicated contact person, 7×24-hour phone
- **Response Time**: 4-hour response for emergency issues
- **Support Scope**: Custom development, on-site deployment, training certification, system integration
- **Charging Method**: Project-based charging

## Service Productization

### 1. Basic Support Package
- **Price**: $700/year
- **Included**:
  - Self-service + remote assistance (working hours)
  - 5 technical consultations
  - 24-hour response
- **Suitable For**: Small projects, customers new to sfsDb

### 2. Advanced Support Package
- **Price**: $2,100/year
- **Included**:
  - Self-service + 7×24-hour remote assistance + regular health checks
  - Unlimited technical consultations
  - 4-hour response
  - 2 on-site supports (arrival within 48 hours in major domestic cities)
- **Suitable For**: Medium-sized projects, customers with higher service quality requirements

### 3. Enterprise Support Package
- **Price**: Starting from $7,100/year
- **Included**:
  - All remote services + on-site support + customized remote management solutions
  - 7×24-hour dedicated engineer
  - Arrival within 24 hours in major domestic cities, 48-72 hours in other areas
- **Suitable For**: Large projects, critical business systems, customers with extremely high service requirements

## Value-added Services

### 1. Health Check
- **Content**: Regular (e.g., quarterly) system health check reports for customers, identifying potential risks
- **Charging**: Can be purchased as an independent service, or included in Advanced and Enterprise Support Packages

### 2. Disaster Recovery Plan
- **Content**: Design data backup and disaster recovery plans for customers
- **Charging**: Priced based on plan complexity and implementation difficulty

### 3. Performance Optimization
- **Content**: Provide database performance tuning services for customer-specific scenarios
- **Charging**: Priced based on optimization scope and complexity

## Service Process

### 1. Service Request Submission
- **Community Service**: Submit through GitHub Issues
- **Professional and Enterprise Services**: Submit through email, phone, or dedicated contact person

### 2. Issue Classification and Assignment
- The technical support team classifies and prioritizes issues
- Assign to corresponding technical personnel based on issue type and service level

### 3. Issue Resolution
- **Remote Priority**: First resolve issues through remote methods
- **On-site Support**: Provide on-site support for complex issues or issues that cannot be resolved remotely

### 4. Service Tracking and Feedback
- All service requests have detailed tracking records
- Customer satisfaction survey after service completion
- Regular analysis of service data, continuous optimization of service processes
